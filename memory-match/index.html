How to play Memory Match

Memory Match is the classic concentration game: a grid of face-down tiles hides matching pairs of symbols. Flip two tiles at a time. If they match, they stay face up. If they don't, they flip back after a moment so you can try again. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert to change the grid size, and clear the whole board using as few moves as possible.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress or score uploaded anywhere?

No. Memory Match runs entirely in your browser. Your move count and best score are stored only in your browser's local storage, and nothing is sent to a server.

Does the game save my best score?

Yes, if your browser allows local storage. Your fewest moves are saved separately for each difficulty level, so you can try to beat your own best at Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert. If local storage is blocked, the game still works, it just will not remember your best scores.

Can I play Memory Match with a keyboard only?

Yes. Tab moves between tiles and Enter or Space flips the focused tile. A visible focus outline shows which tile is selected at every step.

Is there a time limit?

No. Memory Match tracks how long you take and how many moves you use, but there is no countdown and no penalty for taking your time.
